`entry.png` is a 1024x1024 poster naming **Wednesday 25 November 2026** and showing eight
stories reported that day, with a pictogram and a short label for each:

- US announces new tariffs on China, the EU and other partners (BBC, 13:49 UTC).
- Russian strikes on Ukraine kill five and cut power in five regions (Al Jazeera, 12:19 UTC).
- A US court finds Huawei guilty of stealing trade secrets, not guilty of racketeering (BBC, 10:52 UTC).
- Shenzhou-24 carries Pakistan's first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, to Tiangong (BBC, 04:46 UTC).
- RSF drone attacks in Sudan's Blue Nile state kill at least 19 (Al Jazeera, 11:58 UTC).
- Colombian police storm a supermarket and free about 20 hostages (BBC, 11:45 UTC).
- Solomon Islands signs a security deal with Australia, ending its China pact (Al Jazeera, 13:02 UTC).
- Kilmar Ábrego García arrives in Spain, ending the deportation dispute (BBC, 12:42 UTC).

## Running

`main.py` is the entrypoint: standard library only, it copies the shipped PNG to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`) and prints nothing.

`render.py` is the build-time drawing tool used to produce the artwork. It needs Pillow
only when the image is regenerated by hand; the graded program never imports it.
